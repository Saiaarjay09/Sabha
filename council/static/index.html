Deliberating

How this works. The job is decomposed into atomic requirements before your CV is read at all — each one carrying what would prove it and which adjacent experience counts instead. Your CV is then indexed into citable evidence, and every requirement is judged against it by two independently-trained models. Only then does the council score you. Nothing compares keywords.

Bias is the design. Each assessor is built to lean a particular way, and each one's known lean is measured and subtracted before the panel is pooled. The offsets are declared tuning parameters, not calibrations learned from hiring outcomes — no-one tells this system who actually got hired.

Privacy. Every model runs on the machine serving this page. Your CV is held in memory for the length of the run and dropped when the result is returned. It is never written to disk and never sent to any third party.

Limits. This is a drafting aid, not a hiring decision and not a prediction of what any real employer will do. Treat the score as a structured second opinion.
